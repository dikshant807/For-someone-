For Nikita 🌙

I don’t even know if you remember it… But in first year, we made eye contact once. Maybe for you it was just a normal moment, but for me, it became one of the most beautiful memories of my life.
At first, I thought it was just attraction. Because honestly, you looked so stunning… so beautiful. But as the semesters passed, I realized it was more than that.
Somewhere in between those days, you slowly made a place in my heart.
I always wanted to talk to you properly, but whenever you came in front of me, my mind just stopped working. I forgot every sentence I ever prepared.
Vo samne thi par hum palakhe uthaa na sake, Pyar to bahot krte hai par kabhi bata na sake. Pass nhi ja skte the kyuki unki tasveer thi ankho me, Bas yahi sochkar hum unse najre mila na sake.
Whenever life felt heavy or messed up, one message from you was enough to make me feel okay again.
I know making a website to express feelings sounds a little weird. But I’m a shy person. And this was probably the only way I could say everything without forgetting my words.
I’m not asking for anything from you. I just wanted you to know what you meant to me.
And before this page ends, I just want one promise from you: Never waste your feelings on someone who doesn’t value your heart. You deserve someone who truly cares for you.
And whenever life feels heavy, whenever you feel low or alone… just text me. I’ll always be there for you.
